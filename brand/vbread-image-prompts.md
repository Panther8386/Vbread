# Vbread — Image generation prompt set

Mode used: built-in image generation.

## 1. Refined logo system

Create a precise flat vector-style identity board for “Vbread”. Use a bold uppercase V built from two bread-like strokes; the right stroke resembles a Vietnamese baguette with exactly three diagonal cuts. Show horizontal and stacked lockups, icon-only, one-color, reversed, and 48 px preview. Palette: Crust Red #C83B22, Pickle Green #2F6B45, Paper White #FFF8E8, Charcoal Ink #20201D. Keep it reproducible in one ink and avoid gradients, shadows, chef hats, wheat, croissants, serif typography, and extra text.

## 2. Physical applications

Show a realistic Vietnamese mobile bánh mì cart branded “Vbread”, using the bread-V symbol and a sturdy condensed wordmark. Include paper bread sleeve, carry bag, dark-green apron, and sidewalk banner. Use Crust Red as the dominant outdoor color, Pickle Green as secondary, Paper White and Charcoal Ink. Set the scene on a sunny Vietnamese sidewalk and keep all materials practical, inexpensive, and highly visible. Required copy: “Vbread” and “Bánh mì 25.000đ”.

## 3. Internal mobile PWA

Create a high-fidelity mobile operations dashboard for Vbread staff. Show shift status, cart/location, revenue, inventory alerts, and large quick actions. Use Be Vietnam Pro headings, IBM Plex Sans body, IBM Plex Mono numbers, Crust Red primary, Pickle Green status, Paper White background and Charcoal text. Required Vietnamese labels include “Ca sáng”, “Đang bán”, “Doanh thu”, “2.450.000 ₫”, “Tồn kho”, “Tạo đơn”, and “Chốt ca”. Avoid generic fintech styling, gradients, glassmorphism, oversized rounded cards, emoji, and customer-ordering flows.
